RV32I core, RTL to sky130 with scan-based DFT

Sep 2026 — ongoing
SystemVerilog · cocotb · Yosys · sky130 · Python
Independent project

A five-stage pipelined RISC-V core taken from RTL through synthesis to a sky130 layout, then attacked with a scan chain, a fault simulator and an ATPG engine written from scratch.

5,986standard cells
0.08mm² die area
15.8kfaults targeted
514test patterns
Stuck-at fault coverage0.00%
How it was built
  • Five-stage pipelined RV32I core with forwarding, load-use stall and branch flush, beside a lint-clean single-cycle reference core.
  • A cocotb suite backed by Python reference models, 13 directed programs assembled by an RV32I assembler I wrote, and 10 hazard tests.
  • Synthesis to sky130 surfaced two RTL patterns that both lint and simulation had signed off on.
  • Scan insertion, bit-parallel stuck-at fault simulation and two-phase ATPG — random fill, then PODEM — written from scratch.

QKD protocols on photonic devices

Aug — Dec 2025
Photonics · optical fibre · systems costing
Supervised by Dr. Rahul Singhal, BITS Pilani

Evaluated hardware architectures for BB84, B92 and E91 quantum key distribution over optical fibre, then proposed a minimum-cost build that survives real components.

What the study covered
  • Evaluated hardware architectures for the BB84, B92 and E91 protocols in optical fibre systems.
  • Compared trade-offs across source preparation, basis selection and detection, each with a cost attached rather than treated as ideal.
  • Proposed a minimum-cost BB84 implementation optimised for practical deployment rather than ideal-component performance.

Demand-side energy management for agrophotovoltaic systems

Jan — Jul 2025
Python · particle swarm optimisation · real tariff data
Supervised by Dr. Sumanta Pasari, BITS Pilani

A particle swarm optimiser that maximises energy-trading revenue for agrivoltaic prosumers under real grid and tariff constraints.

How the model works
  • Built a particle swarm optimiser for agrivoltaic prosumers under grid and tariff constraints, driven by real-world data rather than synthetic profiles.
  • Maximised energy-trading revenue across the dispatch schedule, with the constraint set doing the work a penalty term usually fudges.
  • Evaluated how the model performs and scales for actual deployment, not only on the training window.

Statistical distributions for energy data analysis

Jan — Jul 2024
Python · estimation theory · goodness-of-fit testing
Supervised by Dr. Sumanta Pasari, BITS Pilani

Fitted exponential-family distributions to solar and wind data, then benchmarked three estimators against each other to see which survived the tails.

What the comparison showed
  • Modelled solar and wind datasets with exponential-family distributions to capture their stochastic behaviour rather than smoothing it away.
  • Benchmarked method of moments, maximum likelihood and L-moments estimators on the same data using statistical fit metrics.
  • Drew out what the fits imply for uncertainty modelling and grid-level forecasting — the groundwork the optimiser above stands on.
