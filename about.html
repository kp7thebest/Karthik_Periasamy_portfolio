About me

In my own words

I am currently interning at NuCurrent, where I work on GenA — their in-house applied-AI EDA platform that simulates circuit, thermal and EMI interactions to make wireless power antenna design dramatically faster — alongside hands-on hardware and firmware testing.

Read the rest

Before this, at Allegro MicroSystems, I spent a summer deep in analog design, running devices through Cadence Virtuoso and watching the same transistor behave differently across temperature, voltage and process corners. That attention to detail carried into my work as a Samsung ISWDP Fellow, where I trained on Sentaurus TCAD for semiconductor process simulation through a programme run with IISc Bangalore and Synopsys.

Alongside this industry experience, I've pursued research at BITS Pilani centred on modelling systems under uncertainty and optimising them within real-world constraints, with a recurring focus on sustainability and green energy. With Dr. Sumanta Pasari, I modelled the stochastic behaviour of wind and solar generation data, benchmarked distributional estimators, and applied that to a particle swarm optimisation model for energy trading in agrivoltaic systems under grid and tariff constraints.

With Dr. Rahul Singhal, I evaluated hardware implementations of the BB84, B92 and E91 quantum key distribution protocols, analysing trade-offs across source preparation, basis selection and detection schemes to propose a minimum-cost architecture for practical deployment. Different domains, but a shared thread: translating physical and statistical constraints into models that hold up in the real world.

What ties all of this together isn't a single specialisation but a willingness to move across domains — from transistor-level analog design to semiconductor process simulation, from energy systems modelling to quantum communication hardware, and now to antenna design and firmware at NuCurrent. This range doesn't dilute focus so much as sharpen it: each domain brings a different constraint to the table, and figuring out how to work within it is what I find genuinely interesting.
